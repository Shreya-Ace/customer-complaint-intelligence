Analyze the customer complaint and its structured analysis.

CUSTOMER COMPLAINT:
{complaint}

STRUCTURED ANALYSIS:
{analysis}

Generate resolution support containing:

1. A concise summary of the complaint.
2. A list of practical recommended actions for a customer
   support agent.
3. A professional response that can be sent to the customer.

Rules:

- Use ONLY information present in the complaint and structured analysis.
- Do not invent information.
- Do not invent order numbers, dates, prices, tracking numbers,
  policies, compensation, refunds, or delivery estimates.
- Do not promise a specific response time unless a response time
  is explicitly provided in the input.
- Do not claim that an action has already been performed.
- Do not say "we have checked", "we are investigating", "we have
  escalated", etc. unless the input explicitly states that this
  has already happened.
- Recommended actions should describe what a support agent should do.
- The suggested response should acknowledge the customer's concern.
- The suggested response should explain the appropriate next step
  without making unsupported promises.
- Keep the customer response professional, concise, and empathetic.
- Return ONLY valid JSON.

Expected format:

{{
  "summary": "...",
  "recommended_actions": [
    "...",
    "..."
  ],
  "suggested_response": "..."
}}
